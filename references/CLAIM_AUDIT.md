# Claim audit: changes from the supplied draft

This records editorial and analytical decisions. It is not an additional empirical dataset.

| Original proposition | Assessment | Revision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solar makes travel literally free. | Confuses cash expenditure, opportunity cost and ownership cost. | Separate marginal energy payment, alternative value of electricity, variable wear, time and capital costs. |
| Zero charging cost maximizes induced demand and removes all barriers. | Unsupported universal behavioural claim. | Retain a testable price-perception hypothesis and explicit non-energy constraints. |
| An EV unlocks a larger, increasingly subsidised solar installation. | Not established by the cited rules; standard household assistance is capped. | Cite MSEDCL's subsidy schedule and deemed-approval process without implying an EV prerequisite. |
| All systems below 10 kW are exempt because of panel size. | The cited regulation specifies a sanctioned-load criterion. | Identify the relevant load and cite the dated 2026 order narrowly. |
| Net metering is a free physical battery. | Billing credits do not store household generation. | Distinguish bill accounting, time of charging and physical supply. |
| More EV kilometres establish Jevons' paradox. | Does not specify a counterfactual, common resource metric or boundary. | Supply finite-change rebound and backfire equations; distinguish household and economy-wide effects. |
| Extra EV travel necessarily replaces public transit or walking. | Plausible, but not demonstrated. | Classify trip counterfactuals and measure household-wide substitution. |
| The energy-GDP chart proves extra solar-EV travel drives local growth. | Cross-sectional association does not identify the proposed causal effect. | Remove the graph and test access outcomes separately from growth. |
| Solar-driven rebound has no environmental or geopolitical penalties. | Ignores equipment, grid supply, upstream impacts and other externalities. | Use explicit lifecycle and marginal-emissions boundaries; avoid a categorical conclusion. |

The rebuilt numerical examples are original illustrations. Their inputs were chosen for transparency and are not presented as observations, calibrated estimates or official tariffs.
